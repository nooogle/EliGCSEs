## Paper 2 — Modern Texts and Poetry

### Section A: Modern Text (prose or drama)
- Choice of two questions on the chosen text; answer one
- Essay-style, no extract provided

## Assessment Objectives

| AO | Description | Weighting |
|----|-------------|-----------|
| AO1 | Read, understand and respond; maintain critical style; use quotations to support interpretations | 37.5% |
| AO2 | Analyse language, form and structure; use appropriate terminology | 42.5% |
| AO3 | Understand how texts relate to their historical and cultural contexts | 15% |
| AO4 | Vocabulary, sentence structure, spelling and punctuation | 5% |

**AO2 is the biggest mark-earner (42.5%).** Precise language analysis with terminology is essential.  
**AO3 context** is only 15% — relevant context should be woven in, not front-loaded.  
**AO4** is assessed on one question per paper only.

---

## What Examiners Want

- **AO1:** Personal, critical response with a clear argument. Quotes embedded naturally, not just dropped in.
- **AO2:** Analyse *effects* of specific language/structural choices — not just identify techniques ("Shakespeare uses a metaphor to...").
- **AO3:** Context integrated into analysis, not listed as background. Show how context *shapes* the text or a character.
- Avoid plot retelling. Examiners reward interpretation, not summary.
