# Poetry Practice Questions — Worlds and Lives

**Paper:** 2, Section B  
**Marks:** 30  
**Suggested time:** 30 minutes per question  
**Format:** Closed book — you must quote from memory

---

## How the exam question works

AQA names **one poem** in the question. You choose **one other poem** from the cluster to compare it with.

**Typical wording:**
> "Compare how poets present [theme] in **[named poem]** and one other poem from the anthology."

The best second poem to choose is the one where you can make the most **interesting comparison** — not necessarily the most obvious match, but the one you know best and can quote accurately.

---

## How to structure your answer (30 minutes)

| Paragraph | What to do |
|-----------|------------|
| **Intro** | Name both poems and briefly state how each treats the theme differently |
| **Para 1** | Close analysis of a quote from the named poem — language, structure, effect (AO2). Weave in relevant context (AO3) |
| **Para 2** | Close analysis of a quote from your chosen poem — language, structure, effect. Link back to Para 1 with a comparison word |
| **Para 3** | A second point of comparison — zoom into a different quote from each poem |
| **Conclusion** | Summarise the key similarity and difference; comment on overall effect |

**Key comparison words:** similarly / in contrast / whereas / both poets / unlike / Femi echoes Dharker's... / where Robinson focuses on... Wordsworth instead...

**Remember:** AO2 is 42.5% of marks. Don't just identify techniques — always explain the *effect* on the reader.

## Practice Questions

### Q1
Compare how poets present **feelings of belonging** in **'Homing'** by Liz Berry and one other poem from the anthology.

*Suggested pairings: 'A Portable Paradise', 'Name Journeys', 'Lines Written in Early Spring'*

## When you paste your answer in for review

To get useful feedback, paste:

1. **The question you answered**
2. **Your answer in full**
3. Optionally: which second poem you chose and why

Feedback will cover:
- **AO1** — Is your argument clear? Do you have a personal, critical viewpoint?
- **AO2** — Are you analysing *effects* of language, not just naming techniques?
- **AO3** — Is context woven in, or bolted on?
- **Overall** — What to prioritise in the next draft
