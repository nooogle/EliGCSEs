# Revision Priorities

## College Entry Requirement

**5 GCSEs at grade 4 or above, including Mathematics and English Language.**

Grade 4 is the minimum pass (equivalent to the old grade C). Grade 5 is a "strong pass" and may be preferred by some colleges — worth aiming for where possible.
